Website notice updated 12 November 2026 · Android app v1.10.2 / code 8

Pausen is developed by Approxy-dev. It helps you follow a bedtime plan and take breaks from scrolling. This notice explains the Android app’s local data, its permissions, and this website.

What stays on your device

Pausen stores your bedtime and pause settings, chosen and allowed app names, appearance preferences, aggregate scrolling time, pause decisions, and night records in its private local storage. These records are not uploaded to Approxy-dev.

The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. Its Android manifest does not request internet access. Android cloud backup and device-transfer extraction are disabled.

Permissions with a purpose

Accessibility: identifies the foreground app and shows bedtime protection, breathing pauses, and a warm reading tint. During protected periods, it can inspect text in Settings and installer windows to recognize attempts to change protected settings or uninstall Pausen, then return from those screens. That inspection happens on the device; screen text is not sent to the developer.

Device administrator: supports uninstall protection during protected periods. Together with the Accessibility guard, this can make disabling protection or uninstalling less immediate. Review the app’s commitment rules and allowed apps before enabling a plan.

Usage access and display over other apps: provide backup app detection and a shield if the Accessibility service is interrupted.

Notifications, exact alarms, and background battery access: support scheduled transitions and show protection status. Android’s explicit Force stop stops the app, and manufacturer battery management can interrupt protection.

App visibility and audio: installed launcher apps are listed so you can choose what to allow or pause. Media may be quieted during a pause and restored afterward; calls and alarm volume are not changed. Your app list is not uploaded.

Understand your commitment

Applied bedtime plans are fixed in the app. Turning on Scroll Pause starts a 24-hour lock on its settings. Strict mode separately freezes pause settings and allowed apps until its waiting period and explicit off confirmation are complete. These rules are intended to support your own choices.

Settings and uninstall guards operate during protected periods. Pausen is a self-control tool; it does not guarantee uninterrupted protection on every Android device. Plan your allowed apps before committing.

Retention, updates, and deletion

Your local records remain until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Updating with the same application ID and signing certificate preserves compatible local records. The developer has no copy to recover deleted data. Device administration may need to be deactivated before Android permits uninstalling; protected periods can guard that flow.

This website

This site does not include analytics scripts, advertising, third-party fonts, or application cookies. Your browser contacts the website’s host to load the page and download the APK. Hosting providers may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, for delivery and security. The app’s offline design does not mean visiting a website is offline.

The interactive phone and breathing previews run in your browser. They do not change your phone settings or save your input to browser storage.

When you contact us

The feedback form prepares a draft in your email app. It does not send a message automatically or submit your details to a form service. Your email provider processes the message if you choose to send it. The form values remain on the page until you leave or reload it.

Approxy-dev uses support messages to respond to your request. Share only what is needed to describe your issue; do not include passwords or private screen content. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing approxydev@gmail.com.

Changes and questions

If data handling changes, this notice will be updated. For questions, contact approxydev@gmail.com.
